# Professional Experience Case Study
## Moodle LMS Administration, Digital Learning Support and Quality Assurance

**Professional portfolio:** Damaris  
**Organisation:** TEKTAGOGY Group  
**Professional experience:** University-level ICT and digital learning environment, since 2008

## 1. Professional Background

I have professional experience in university-level ICT and digital learning support, with a focus on helping lecturers use Moodle to support teaching, learning and online assessment.

My responsibilities have included lecturer capacity building, Moodle user and access management, technical troubleshooting, student support and support for online assessment activities.

I also bring institutional quality assurance experience, having served as a Team Lead Auditor in internal audits conducted under an ISO 9001:2015 quality management framework.

## 2. Moodle LMS Administration and Lecturer Support

My practical Moodle experience includes supporting lecturers and academic staff in the effective use of the LMS.

Key areas of involvement include:

- Supporting lecturers in uploading learning materials and resources.
- Assisting with Moodle quiz configuration.
- Supporting assignment setup and online assessment activities.
- Conducting Moodle training for lecturers and academic staff.
- Managing Moodle users and access permissions.
- Troubleshooting LMS-related challenges.
- Providing technical support to students experiencing access and learning-platform difficulties.

### My contribution

My role has involved working directly with academic staff to address practical LMS challenges and strengthen their ability to use digital learning tools.

This experience has provided insight into the relationship between technical LMS administration, lecturer confidence, student access and effective online learning delivery.

## 3. Online Assessment Support

Online assessment is an important area of my Moodle support experience.

My involvement has included:

- Supporting lecturers with quiz setup.
- Assisting with assignment configuration.
- Helping lecturers review assessment settings.
- Troubleshooting student access and quiz-attempt problems.
- Supporting students experiencing difficulties with Moodle assessments.

My approach emphasises checking assessment settings, identifying configuration issues and involving the responsible lecturer where academic decisions are required.

## 4. Lecturer Training and Capacity Building

I have personally conducted Moodle training for lecturers and academic staff.

My support approach focuses on helping users understand and apply Moodle functions in their teaching activities.

Key areas include:

- Navigating Moodle.
- Managing course learning materials.
- Setting up quizzes and assignments.
- Supporting online teaching activities.
- Understanding common LMS challenges.
- Improving confidence in using digital learning technologies.

## 5. Institutional Quality Assurance and ISO 9001:2015 Auditing

In addition to my digital learning experience, I have more than three years of experience as a Team Lead Auditor in internal institutional audits.

I have received formal ISO 9001:2015 Lead Auditor training.

My audit responsibilities and experience include:

- Coordinating internal audit activities.
- Leading audit teams.
- Reviewing processes against applicable institutional requirements.
- Identifying and documenting audit findings and nonconformities.
- Following up corrective actions.
- Supporting continual improvement initiatives.

### Relevance to digital learning

Quality assurance principles are important in LMS administration because digital learning processes require consistency, documented procedures, monitoring and continual improvement.

I apply this quality-focused perspective when considering:

- Moodle course readiness.
- User access and enrolment processes.
- Online assessment configuration.
- Lecturer support procedures.
- LMS troubleshooting and issue resolution.
- Monitoring and improvement of digital learning support processes.

## 6. Practical Professional Approach

My professional approach combines three complementary areas:

**Digital learning support:** Helping lecturers and students use Moodle effectively.

**Technical administration:** Supporting LMS access, course activities, online assessments and troubleshooting.

**Quality assurance:** Applying process-based thinking, evidence review, corrective action and continual improvement.
